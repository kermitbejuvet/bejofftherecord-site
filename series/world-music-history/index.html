WORLD MUSIC HISTORY

World Music History

World Music History tells one country's music in order, one short episode at a time. The first country is Mexico. The series starts in the 1950s with ranchera, bolero and the singers of the cinema golden age, moves through La Nueva Ola and the Mexican rock scene of the 1960s, and reaches the 1970s with norteño, the corrido, cumbia, the balada romántica and Canto Nuevo. Each era gets a context episode, then spotlights on the artists who defined it, often followed by a top 10 of their songs.

Frontwoman

Frontwoman is a multi-part biography series about women who led rock bands, and about the gap between what they made and what was written about them. Each artist gets several episodes: where she came from, the sound she made, how the press and the industry treated her, and what she left behind. So far: Debbie Harry of Blondie, Brody Dalle of The Distillers, and Shirley Manson of Garbage.

Taking It Off The Shelf

Taking It Off The Shelf revisits 2000s British indie bands you might have stopped listening to. Each episode plays the most popular song from every studio album, in order, so you hear where the band went after the hit you remember. So far: The Fratellis, The Wombats, Kasabian, Kaiser Chiefs and Bloc Party.

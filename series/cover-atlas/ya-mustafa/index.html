Ya Mustafa: one Egyptian song, eight countries

"Ya Mustafa" is an Egyptian song. Bob Azzam's French version became a European hit in 1960, and the same year it was recorded in Spanish, Danish and Japanese, followed by German, Serbo-Croatian, Catalan and, in 1994, Hindi.

The story

"Ya Mustafa" mixes Arabic, French and Italian in one chorus, which is a clue to where it came from: the cosmopolitan Egypt of the 1950s. The episode opens with the Egyptian recording by Mohamed Fawzi.

Europe heard it through Bob Azzam, a singer of Lebanese origin who grew up in Egypt. His French version "Mustapha" was a hit in 1960. The same year José Guardiola sang it in Spanish, a Danish film used it, and in Japan Kyu Sakamoto recorded it as "Kanashiki rokujussai".

Then it kept going: Leo Leandros in German, Đorđe Marjanović in Yugoslavia in 1961, Rudy Ventura in Catalan, and decades later the Hindi film Aatish in 1994. Eight countries in the video, and Catalonia on top.

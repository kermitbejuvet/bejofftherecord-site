Tombe la neige: the versions around the world

"Tombe la neige" is a 1963 ballad by Salvatore Adamo, the Sicilian-born singer who grew up in Belgium. Adamo recorded it himself in Italian, German, Spanish and Japanese, and it was covered in Turkish by Ajda Pekkan and in Korean.

The story

Salvatore Adamo was born in Sicily in 1943 and moved to Belgium as a child, where his father worked in the coal mines. He wrote and sang in French, and "Tombe la neige" made him a star across the French-speaking world in 1963.

What makes this song unusual in Cover Atlas is who carried it abroad. Instead of handing it to local singers, Adamo recorded many of the translations himself: "Cade la neve" in Italian, a German version, "Cae la nieve" in Spanish and a Japanese version, where the song became one of his best known.

Other singers took it further. Ajda Pekkan recorded it in Turkish as "Her Yerde Kar Var", and a Korean version followed. In every language the image stays the same: snow falling, and someone who is not coming.

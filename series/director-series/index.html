Director Series

The Director Series is about the people behind the camera of the music video era. Each director gets a spotlight and a top 10 of their videos: Chris Cunningham, Diane Martel, Mary Lambert, Joseph Kahn and Dave Meyers, plus specials on film directors who made music videos, musicians who directed their own, and Spike Lee's music videos.

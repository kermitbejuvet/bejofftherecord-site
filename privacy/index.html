Last updated: 14 November 2026

When you visit the site, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, the page requested and the time of the request in server logs, to deliver the site and keep it secure. 3. Visitor statistics (GoatCounter)

To see which pages are read, this site uses GoatCounter (goatcounter.com), an open source analytics service with servers in the EU. GoatCounter sets no cookies, stores no IP addresses and does not track you across websites. It only counts page views, the referring site, browser, screen size and country. Unique visits are estimated with a short-lived, non-identifiable hash. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing which content is read (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). No information is stored on your device, so no consent banner is needed.

Videos are shown as a preview image first. The preview images are hosted on this website. Only when you click "Play video" is the player loaded: for most videos the YouTube player in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, otherwise the TikTok player of TikTok Technology Limited, 10 Earlsfort Terrace, Dublin 2, Ireland. The provider then receives data such as your IP address and may set cookies or similar technologies, and may transfer data to the USA. By clicking, you consent to this (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). Links to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube are ordinary links; the respective provider's privacy policy applies once you open them.

All fonts are hosted on this website. No connection to Google Fonts or other font services is made.

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR), and the right to withdraw consent (for example to the video player) at any time.

Lambada isn't Brazilian. It's Bolivian.

The song the world knows as "Lambada" started as "Llorando se fue", recorded by the Bolivian group Los Kjarkas in 1981. It has since been recorded in Peru, Brazil, France, Egypt, Japan, South Korea, Turkey, Hong Kong and India.

The story

Most people remember Lambada as the sound of Brazil in 1989. The melody is older. Los Kjarkas, a folk group from Bolivia, recorded "Llorando se fue" in 1981. A Peruvian cumbia version followed, then a Brazilian one, and in 1989 the French-Brazilian group Kaoma turned it into a worldwide hit.

Kaoma's single credited a pseudonym, not Los Kjarkas. The original writers took legal action and won. Meanwhile the melody kept moving, into Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Turkish, Cantonese and Hindi.
